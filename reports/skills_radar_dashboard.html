The Core Finding

Kenya is a different market, not a delayed one. The trickle-down thesis—that global skill demand predicts Kenya’s with a lag—does not hold. Same-year rank correlation is only +0.25 (weak). One-year lag is −0.20 (worse than chance). Of skills that rose globally in 2024–2025, 82.6% also rose in Kenya—but 74.2% of all skills rose in Kenya, so the predictive lift is just +8.5 percentage points.
